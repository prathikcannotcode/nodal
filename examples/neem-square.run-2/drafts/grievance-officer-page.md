## How to complain

Use the [report form](/help/report), or email the Grievance Officer. Tell us:

- the link to the post or profile, or where you saw it;
- what is wrong with it;
- whether you are the person affected or are complaining for them;
- an email address where we can reply.

You can complain about any content on Neem Square, or about how we applied our rules to you.

## What happens next

Every complaint gets a ticket number. Keep it: you can check progress at /help/report/status.

| Your complaint | What we do | By when |
|---|---|---|
| Any complaint | Acknowledge it, with your ticket number | Within 24 hours (rule 3(2)(a)(i), `IT-3-2-a-ack`) |
| Content that shows you, or someone you represent, nude, partly nude or in a sexual act, or that impersonates you, including a morphed or AI-made image | Remove it or block access to it | Within 2 hours of your complaint (rule 3(2)(b), `IT-3-2-b`) |
| A request to remove content that breaks our rules, other than a copyright or other intellectual property claim, a claim that the content belongs to you, or a claim that it simply breaks a law | Decide and act | Within 36 hours of your report (rule 3(2)(a)(i), `IT-3-2-a-removal`) |
| Any other complaint | Decide and tell you why | Within 7 days of receiving it (rule 3(2)(a)(i), `IT-3-2-a-resolve`) |

## If you disagree with our decision

You can appeal to the Grievance Appellate Committee at https://gac.gov.in within 30 days of receiving our decision (rule 3A(3), `IT-3A-3`). You can also appeal if we do not resolve your complaint in time.
