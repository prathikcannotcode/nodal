Grievance redressal

If you have a complaint about content on Neem Square, or about how we have applied our rules, our grievance team will look into it.

How to reach us

Email grievance@neemsquare.example or use the report form. You do not need an account.

What happens next

  1. You get an acknowledgement with a ticket number within 24 hours.
  2. We look into it and write back with our decision and the reasons.
  3. If you disagree with our decision, you can appeal to the Grievance Appellate Committee.
