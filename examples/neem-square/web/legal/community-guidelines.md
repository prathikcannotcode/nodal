# Community Guidelines

Neem Square is for sharing what you make and what you care about. These guidelines sit alongside our [Terms](/terms).

- **Be yourself.** No impersonation, no fake accounts.
- **No nudity or sexual content.** Never share intimate images of anyone without their consent.
- **Keep children safe.** We report child sexual abuse material to the authorities.
- **No hate or harassment.**
- **No scams, gambling promotions or money laundering.**
- **Respect other people's work.**
- **AI images.** Keep the "AI-generated" mark on images made with Imagine.
- **Follow the law.**

Report anything that breaks these guidelines with the [report form](/help/report).
