# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 March 2026

This policy explains what Neem Square Private Limited collects, why, and your choices.

## What we collect

Your email, phone number, date of birth, profile details, the content you post, and device and usage information.

## Why

To run the service, keep it safe, show advertising and meet legal obligations.

## How long we keep it

We keep your account information while your account is open. When you close your account we delete it within 30 days.

## Your choices

You can edit your profile, download your data and close your account in Settings.
