# Runbook: information requests from government agencies

Owner: Trust and Safety Operations (the nodal contact person). Last reviewed: April 2026.

## Where they arrive

Email to `lea@neemsquare.example`, paged to the `lea-liaison` rota at all hours (see `config/oncall.yaml`).

## Steps

1. Check the request is a written order from an agency authorised to make it, and that it states its purpose. If not, write back the same day asking for one.
2. Log it in the requests register with the time received.
3. Pull only the information the order names. A second person on the rota reviews the export.
4. Send the response through the agency's official channel and record the time sent.

## Clock

Respond as soon as possible and no later than 72 hours after receiving the order. The rota lead gets a page at 48 hours if the request is still open.

## Emergencies

A request that cites a threat to life is handled at once by whoever is on the rota, with the paperwork to follow.
